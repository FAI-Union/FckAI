# PP-2026-001 — Mandatory public disclosure of AI compute energy use

**Status:** draft for discussion · **Opened:** 2026-11-26

## Problem / 问题

Energy and water costs of training and inference are borne by the public (grid capacity, prices, emissions) while the data needed to evaluate those costs is not public. Affected communities cannot weigh trade-offs without measurements.

## Proposed measure / 建议措施

Require large model developers and data-centre operators to publish, per reporting period:

1. Total electricity consumed, split into training and inference where feasible.
2. Water usage and cooling method.
3. Energy source mix (renewable / gas / grid average).
4. Method and estimation uncertainty, so figures are comparable.

Disclosure should be machine-readable and archived, not a one-off press release.

## Evidence base / 证据基础

- [EV-2026-001](../RESEARCH/EV-2026-001.md) — IEA projects global data-centre electricity demand above 945 TWh by 2030, AI the main driver.

## Trade-offs / 利弊

- **For:** informed public debate; comparability; early warning for grid planning.
- **Against / risks:** reporting burden on smaller players; commercially sensitive detail; estimates may be noisy. Mitigation: thresholds by scale, report ranges and methods, protect trade secrets while publishing aggregates.
