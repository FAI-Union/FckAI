FAI · Human-First AI Accountability

Open. Non-commercial. Evidence-first.

FAI is an open-source AI accountability evidence archive. We collect sourced evidence on AI ethics, training-data consent, AI energy cost, job displacement and deepfake accountability, so decisions that affect people can be examined, debated and appealed. We welcome AI tools; we oppose unconstrained deployment that makes people pay the price.

A featured piece of evidence

EV-2026-001 · Energy and environmental cost

Claim: Global data-centre electricity demand is projected to more than double by 2030, to around 945 TWh — slightly more than Japan's entire current consumption. AI is identified as the main driver.

This is a projection, not a measured value. The source explicitly notes uncertainty in adoption speed and efficiency gains.

Source: International Energy Agency, Energy and AI, 2025-04-10 · Full evidence card

Recent evidence cards

EV-2026-008 · Generative AI and jobs

The ILO–NASK Global Index finds 1 in 4 jobs worldwide potentially exposed to generative AI — with transformation, not replacement, the most likely outcome.

Source: ILO, 2025-05-20 · Evidence card

EV-2026-017 · Deepfake transparency

Article 50 of the EU AI Act requires providers of systems generating synthetic audio, image, video or text to make outputs machine-readable and detectable as artificially generated or manipulated.

Source: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 · Evidence card
