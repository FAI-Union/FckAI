EV-2026-001 · Energy and environmental cost

Claim: Global data-centre electricity demand is projected to more than double by 2030, to around 945 TWh — slightly more than Japan's entire current consumption. AI is identified as the main driver.

This is a projection, not a measured value. The source explicitly notes uncertainty in adoption speed and efficiency gains.
