# EV-2026-006 — US Copyright Office says economists have only begun to map AI's copyright-policy implications

## Claim / 主张

In February 2025 the US Copyright Office released an agenda-setting publication produced by economic scholars stating that AI development has meaningful implications for the economic frameworks of copyright policy, while taking no institutional position on the proposals.

2025 年 2 月，美国版权局发布由经济学者撰写的议题框架，指出 AI 发展对版权政策的经济框架具有实质影响，但该局对其中观点不持机构立场。

## Source / 来源

- Original: [USCO Releases Publication on the Economic Implications of AI for Copyright Policy](https://copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1062.html)
- Publisher: U.S. Copyright Office
- Publication date: 2025-02-12

## Excerpt / 引述

> "Development of AI technology has meaningful implications for the economic frameworks of copyright policy, and economists have only just begun to explore those."

## Caveats / 说明

- This is an agenda-setting framework by an external scholar group, not a legal holding or Office policy position.
- The announcement explicitly states the Office does not take a position on the ideas for this project.
- Do not read this card as evidence of economic harm magnitude.
