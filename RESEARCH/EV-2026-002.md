# EV-2026-002 — US Copyright Office: commercial AI training that competes with source works exceeds fair-use boundaries

## Claim / 主张

The US Copyright Office concluded in its May 2025 pre-publication report that some generative-AI training may be transformative, but commercial use of large collections of copyrighted works to produce competing expressive content — especially via illegal access — goes beyond established fair-use boundaries.

美国版权局在 2025 年 5 月预发布报告中认为：部分生成式 AI 训练可能具有转化性，但以大量受版权保护作品训练、产出与原件竞争的商业表达内容（尤其涉及非法获取时）超出既有的合理使用边界。

## Source / 来源

- Original: [Copyright and Artificial Intelligence, Part 3: Generative AI Training (Pre-Publication Version)](https://copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-3-Generative-AI-Training-Report-Pre-Publication-Version.pdf)
- Publisher: U.S. Copyright Office (Library of Congress)
- Publication date: 2025-05-09

## Excerpt / 引述

> "making commercial use of vast troves of copyrighted works to produce expressive content that competes with them in existing markets, especially where this is accomplished through illegal access, goes beyond established fair use boundaries."

## Caveats / 说明

- This is a **pre-publication** version; the Office states it cannot prejudge any particular case.
- On licensing, the same report treats government intervention as premature because voluntary licensing is growing.
- No conclusion about any specific lawsuit, company or model is made here.
