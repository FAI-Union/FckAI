# EV-2026-013 — Google reports a 12% cut in data-centre energy emissions in 2024

## Claim / 主张

Google's 2025 Environmental Report states that the company reduced data-centre energy emissions by 12% in 2024 despite increased energy demand.

谷歌《2025 环境报告》称，尽管能源需求上升，2024 年数据中心能源排放仍下降了 12%。

## Source / 来源

- Original: [Google 2025 Environmental Report](https://sustainability.google/google-2025-environmental-report)
- Publisher: Google LLC
- Publication date: 2025-06

## Excerpt / 引述

> "In 2024, we reduced our data center energy emissions by 12%, even in the face of increased energy demands."

## Caveats / 说明

- Self-reported; covers data-centre energy emissions only, not total company greenhouse-gas emissions.
- Separate reporting has noted Google's overall emissions rose substantially versus 2019.
- The reduction does not establish that absolute electricity use fell.
