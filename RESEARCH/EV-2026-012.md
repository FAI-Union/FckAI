# EV-2026-012 — IEA: data centres consumed ~460 TWh in 2022 and could exceed 1,000 TWh in 2026

## Claim / 主张

The IEA estimated global data-centre electricity consumption at 460 TWh in 2022 and projected it could reach more than 1,000 TWh by 2026.

国际能源署估算 2022 年全球数据中心用电量约 460 TWh，并预测 2026 年可能超过 1000 TWh。

## Source / 来源

- Original: [Electricity 2024 — Executive summary](https://www.iea.org/reports/electricity-2024/executive-summary)
- Publisher: International Energy Agency (IEA)
- Publication date: 2024-01

## Excerpt / 引述

> "After globally consuming an estimated 460 terawatt-hours (TWh) in 2022, data centres' total electricity consumption could reach more than 1 000 TWh in 2026."

## Caveats / 说明

- This is a projection, not a measured 2026 value.
- The IEA's later *Energy and AI* report uses a revised scope (415 TWh in 2024); the two figures are not directly comparable.
- The 2024 text bundles AI with crypto and other data-centre demand.
