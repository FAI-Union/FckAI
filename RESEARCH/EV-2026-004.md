# EV-2026-004 — US Copyright Office recommends a federal digital-replica law covering all individuals

## Claim / 主张

The US Copyright Office recommended that Congress enact a new federal law protecting all individuals from the knowing distribution of unauthorised digital replicas, citing gaps in existing legal protections.

美国版权局建议国会制定新的联邦法律，保护所有人免受明知未经授权的数字复制品传播，理由是现行法律保护存在缺口。

## Source / 来源

- Original: [Copyright Office Releases Part 1 of Artificial Intelligence Report, Recommends Federal Digital Replica Law](https://copyright.gov/newsnet/2024/1048.html)
- Publisher: U.S. Copyright Office
- Publication date: 2024-07-31

## Excerpt / 引述

> "Given the gaps in existing legal protections, the Office recommends that Congress enact a new federal law that protects all individuals from the knowing distribution of unauthorized digital replicas."

## Caveats / 说明

- This is a **legislative recommendation**, not enacted law.
- Part 1 concerns realistic but false depictions of individuals, not copyrightability or training use.
- No claim is made that the recommendation has been adopted by Congress.
