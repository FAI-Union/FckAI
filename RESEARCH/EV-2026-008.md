# EV-2026-008 — ILO: one in four jobs worldwide is exposed to generative AI

## Claim / 主张

A 2025 ILO–NASK Global Index study finds that about one in four jobs worldwide is potentially exposed to generative AI, with transformation — not replacement — the most likely outcome.

2025 年 ILO–NASK 全球指数研究显示，全球约四分之一岗位可能受到生成式 AI 影响，最可能的结果是岗位转型而非完全替代。

## Source / 来源

- Original: [One in four jobs at risk of being transformed by GenAI, new ILO–NASK Global Index shows](https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/one-four-jobs-risk-being-transformed-genai-new-ilo%E2%80%93nask-global-index-shows)
- Publisher: International Labour Organization (ILO)
- Publication date: 2025-05-20

## Excerpt / 引述

> "1 in 4 jobs worldwide is potentially exposed to generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) — but that transformation, not replacement, is the most likely outcome."

## Caveats / 说明

- The index measures **potential exposure**, not actual unemployment.
- The ILO notes full job automation remains limited; exposure is higher in high-income countries (34%).
- This card does not claim a net jobs loss.
