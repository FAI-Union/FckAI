# EV-2026-005 — EU DSM Directive lets rightsholders reserve works from text-and-data mining

## Claim / 主张

Article 4 of the EU Digital Single Market Directive provides a text-and-data-mining exception, but it does not apply where rightsholders have expressly reserved the use in an appropriate manner, such as machine-readable means for content made publicly available online.

欧盟《数字单一市场指令》第 4 条设置了文本与数据挖掘例外，但若权利人以适当方式（如对在线公开内容采用机器可读方式）明确保留权利，则该例外不适用。

## Source / 来源

- Original: [Directive (EU) 2019/790 on copyright and related rights in the Digital Single Market](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32019L0790)
- Publisher: European Parliament & Council of the European Union (Official Journal of the EU)
- Publication date: 2019-04-17 (OJ publication 2019-05-17)

## Excerpt / 引述

> "The exception or limitation provided for in paragraph 1 shall not apply where the use of works and other subject matter has been expressly reserved by the rightholders in an appropriate manner, such as machine-readable means [...]"

## Caveats / 说明

- The opt-out limits only the Article 4 exception for lawfully accessed works; it does not itself create an affirmative consent duty.
- What counts as machine-readable reservation varies with national implementation.
- This card describes EU law as written, not enforcement outcomes.
