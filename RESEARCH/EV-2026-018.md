# EV-2026-018 — EU AI Act prohibits real-time remote biometric identification in public spaces for law enforcement, with narrow exceptions

## Claim / 主张

Article 5(1)(h) of the EU AI Act prohibits AI systems used for real-time remote biometric identification of people in publicly accessible spaces for law-enforcement purposes, except where strictly necessary for exhaustively listed objectives.

欧盟《人工智能法案》第 5 条第 1 款第 h 项禁止为执法目的在公共场所实时远程生物识别，除非为法律穷尽列举的目标所严格必需。

## Source / 来源

- Original: [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 5(1)(h)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj)
- Publisher: European Parliament & Council of the European Union
- Publication date: 2024-07-12

## Excerpt / 引述

> "the use of AI systems for real-time remote biometric identification of natural persons in publicly accessible spaces for the purpose of law enforcement, except where and insofar as such use is strictly necessary for one of the following objectives:"

## Caveats / 说明

- The exceptions are narrow: specific missing persons or victims, threats to life or terrorist attack, and serious crimes punishable by at least four years.
- In key cases use must be authorised by a judicial or independent administrative authority.
- This card describes the prohibition, not its enforcement record.
