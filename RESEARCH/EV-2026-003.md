# EV-2026-003 — EU GPAI rules require a copyright policy and a training-content summary from 2 August 2025

## Claim / 主张

Under Article 53 of the EU AI Act, providers of general-purpose AI models must implement a policy respecting EU copyright law and publish a sufficiently detailed summary of the content used for training; the Commission published a template to support this duty from 2 August 2025.

欧盟《人工智能法案》第 53 条要求通用人工智能模型提供者制定尊重欧盟版权法的政策，并发布训练内容摘要；欧盟委员会自 2025 年 8 月 2 日起提供配套模板。

## Source / 来源

- Original: [EU rules on general-purpose AI models start to apply](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/eu-rules-general-purpose-ai-models-start-apply-bringing-more-transparency-safety-and-accountability)
- Publisher: European Commission
- Publication date: 2025-08-01
- Binding text: [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 53](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj)

## Excerpt / 引述

> "From August 2, providers must comply with transparency and copyright obligations when placing GPAI models on the EU market. [...] the Commission published a template to help providers in summarising the data used to train their models."

## Caveats / 说明

- The Commission page is an official summary, not the statutory text; the binding provision is Article 53(1)(c)–(d) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.
- Models placed on the EU market before 2 August 2025 have until 2 August 2027 to comply.
- A published summary is a transparency duty, not proof of consent from every rights holder.
