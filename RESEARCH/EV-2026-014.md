# EV-2026-014 — Peer-reviewed estimate: GPT-3 training used ~5.4 million litres of water

## Claim / 主张

A peer-reviewed study estimates that training GPT-3 in Microsoft's US data centres consumed about 5.4 million litres of water, including 700,000 litres of scope-1 onsite water.

一项同行评议研究估算，在微软美国数据中心训练 GPT-3 消耗约 540 万升水，其中 70 万升为范围一现场用水。

## Source / 来源

- Original: [Making AI Less 'Thirsty', Communications of the ACM 68(7), DOI 10.1145/3724499](https://cacm.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3724499)
- Publisher: Association for Computing Machinery (ACM)
- Publication date: 2025-06-17 (July 2025 issue)

## Excerpt / 引述

> "we show that training GPT-3 in Microsoft's U.S. datacenters can consume a total of 5.4 million liters of water, including 700,000 liters of scope-1 onsite water consumption."

## Caveats / 说明

- The peer-reviewed venue is **CACM**, not Nature; a 2023 arXiv preprint (arXiv:2304.03271) is often cited instead.
- Figures are modelled estimates for one model and specific sites, not metered totals.
- Do not extrapolate to all models or to inference without separate evidence.
