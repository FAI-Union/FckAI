# EV-2026-015 — IEA scenarios: data-centre power emissions 180 Mt today, 300–500 Mt by 2035

## Claim / 主张

The IEA projects emissions from data-centre electricity use rising from about 180 million tonnes today to 300 Mt by 2035 in its Base Case, and up to 500 Mt in its Lift-Off Case.

国际能源署情景分析显示，数据中心用电排放将从目前约 1.8 亿吨升至 2035 年基准情景 3 亿吨、加速情景最高 5 亿吨。

## Source / 来源

- Original: [Energy and AI — Executive summary](https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai/executive-summary)
- Publisher: International Energy Agency (IEA)
- Publication date: 2025-04-10

## Excerpt / 引述

> "Emissions from electricity use by data centres grows from 180 million tonnes (Mt) today to 300 Mt in the Base Case by 2035, and up to 500 Mt in the Lift-Off Case."

## Caveats / 说明

- Scenario analysis to 2035, not a 2030 forecast.
- Covers electricity use only; the IEA frames both scenarios as below 1.5% of total energy-sector emissions.
- No claim is made about emissions from hardware manufacturing or water use.
