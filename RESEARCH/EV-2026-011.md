# EV-2026-011 — CBO says evidence on AI's employment and wage effects remains sparse

## Claim / 主张

The US Congressional Budget Office finds that evidence on AI's effects on employment and wages is sparse and varies by type of AI, with displaced workers potentially facing lower incomes and payroll-tax receipts.

美国国会预算办公室认为，关于 AI 对就业与工资影响的证据仍然稀少且因 AI 类型而异；被替代的劳动者可能面临收入与工薪税收入下降。

## Source / 来源

- Original: [Artificial Intelligence and Its Potential Effects on the Economy and the Federal Budget](https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2024-12/60774-AI-fed-budget.pdf)
- Publisher: Congressional Budget Office (CBO)
- Publication date: 2024-12 (correction noted 2025-05-19)

## Excerpt / 引述

> "Evidence for AI's impact on employment and wages is also sparse and varies by type of AI."

## Caveats / 说明

- CBO explicitly treats direction, magnitude and timing as uncertain; the analysis is qualitative.
- The source is an official CBO PDF; cbo.gov may block automated page fetches.
- This card supports caution, not a specific job-loss number.
