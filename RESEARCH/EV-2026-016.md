# EV-2026-016 — Microsoft signed an 835 MW PPA enabling restart of a retired nuclear plant

## Claim / 主张

Microsoft announced a power purchase agreement with Constellation that would enable the restart of an 835 MW nuclear facility in Pennsylvania retired in 2019.

微软宣布与 Constellation 签署购电协议，支持重启一座 2019 年退役、装机 835 兆瓦的宾夕法尼亚州核设施。

## Source / 来源

- Original: [Accelerating the addition of carbon-free energy: An update on progress](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-cloud/blog/2024/09/20/accelerating-the-addition-of-carbon-free-energy-an-update-on-progress)
- Publisher: Microsoft
- Publication date: 2024-09-20

## Excerpt / 引述

> "Today, we're announcing a power purchase agreement (PPA) with Constellation that will enable the restart of an 835 megawatt (MW) nuclear facility in Pennsylvania that was retired in 2019."

## Caveats / 说明

- This is a restart of an existing plant (Three Mile Island Unit 1 / Crane Clean Energy Center), not new-build capacity.
- The blog does not state the contract term or a guaranteed restart date.
- Procurement commitments do not by themselves prove additional carbon-free supply to the grid.
