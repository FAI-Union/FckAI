# EV-2026-019 — NIST AI RMF 1.0 is voluntary guidance, not a binding rule

## Claim / 主张

NIST released AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 in January 2023 as a voluntary guidance document to help organisations design, develop, deploy and use AI systems while managing AI risks.

美国国家标准与技术研究院于 2023 年 1 月发布 AI 风险管理框架 1.0，作为自愿性指南，帮助组织在设计、开发、部署和使用 AI 系统时管理风险。

## Source / 来源

- Original: [NIST Risk Management Framework Aims to Improve Trustworthiness of Artificial Intelligence](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2023/01/nist-risk-management-framework-aims-improve-trustworthiness-artificial)
- Publisher: U.S. Department of Commerce, National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
- Publication date: 2023-01-26

## Excerpt / 引述

> "a guidance document for voluntary use by organizations designing, developing, deploying or using AI systems to help manage the many risks of AI technologies"

## Caveats / 说明

- Voluntary, non-sector-specific guidance — not a binding regulation or certification scheme.
- NIST has announced revisions as part of the White House AI Action Plan.
- Adoption of the framework does not by itself demonstrate acceptable outcomes.
