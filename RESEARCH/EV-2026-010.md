# EV-2026-010 — OECD: 27% of jobs are in high-automation-risk occupations

## Claim / 主张

The OECD Employment Outlook 2023 reports that, considering all automation technologies including AI, 27% of jobs across OECD countries are in occupations at high risk of automation.

OECD《2023 年就业展望》指出，计入包括 AI 在内的所有自动化技术，OECD 国家 27% 的岗位处于高自动化风险职业中。

## Source / 来源

- Original: [OECD Employment Outlook 2023: Artificial Intelligence and the Labour Market](https://oecd.org/employment-outlook/2023/)
- Publisher: OECD Publishing
- Publication date: 2023-07-11

## Excerpt / 引述

> "When considering all automation technologies including AI, 27% of jobs are in occupations at high-risk of automation."

## Caveats / 说明

- The 27% figure covers **all automation technologies**, not AI alone.
- The report says there is little evidence so far of significant AI-driven employment declines.
- The exposure measure predates the generative-AI wave.
