# EV-2026-007 — IMF: almost 40% of global employment is exposed to AI

## Claim / 主张

IMF staff analysis finds that almost 40% of global employment is exposed to AI, rising to about 60% of jobs in advanced economies.

国际货币基金组织分析认为，全球近 40% 的就业岗位受到 AI 影响；在发达经济体，这一比例约为 60%。

## Source / 来源

- Original: [AI Will Transform the Global Economy. Let's Make Sure It Benefits Humanity](https://www.imf.org/zh/Blogs/Articles/2024/01/14/ai-will-transform-the-global-economy-lets-make-sure-it-benefits-humanity)
- Publisher: International Monetary Fund (IMF)
- Publication date: 2024-01-14

## Excerpt / 引述

> "调查结果令人震惊：全球近40%的就业将受到人工智能的影响。"

## Caveats / 说明

- "Exposure" is not job loss: roughly half of exposed jobs may benefit from AI-driven productivity gains.
- The figure is a staff estimate, not a forecast of unemployment.
