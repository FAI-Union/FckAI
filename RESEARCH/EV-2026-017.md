# EV-2026-017 — EU AI Act requires machine-readable marking of synthetic content

## Claim / 主张

Article 50(2) of the EU AI Act requires providers of AI systems generating synthetic audio, image, video or text content to ensure outputs are marked in a machine-readable format and detectable as artificially generated or manipulated.

欧盟《人工智能法案》第 50 条第 2 款要求生成合成音频、图像、视频或文本的 AI 系统提供者，确保输出以机器可读格式标记，并可被识别为人工生成或篡改。

## Source / 来源

- Original: [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act), Article 50](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R1689)
- Publisher: European Parliament & Council of the European Union (OJ / EUR-Lex)
- Publication date: 2024-07-12 (act dated 2024-06-13)

## Excerpt / 引述

> "Providers of AI systems, including general-purpose AI systems, generating synthetic audio, image, video or text content, shall ensure that the outputs of the AI system are marked in a machine-readable format and detectable as artificially generated or manipulated."

## Caveats / 说明

- This is the **provider** duty; deployer disclosure of deepfakes is a separate paragraph, Article 50(4).
- Article 50(4) carries exceptions including law enforcement and artistic or fictional works.
- The obligation is on marking/detectability, not on proving every downstream use is labelled.
