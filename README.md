# FAI · Human-First AI Accountability

**An open-source AI accountability evidence archive: sourced evidence on AI ethics, training-data consent, AI energy cost, job displacement and deepfake accountability — non-commercial, checkable, public.**

We welcome AI tools. We oppose unconstrained deployment that makes people pay the price. FAI turns individual experiences into traceable public evidence, so decisions about AI can be examined, debated and appealed.

**Your 30 seconds:** Star this repo → [claim a language](LANGUAGES.md#priority-languages) → [submit one sourced evidence card](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/evidence.md). That is the whole loop.

[Read the manifesto](MANIFESTO.md) · [Browse the AI ethics evidence index](RESEARCH/README.md) · [Join the discussion](https://github.com/FAI-Union/FckAI/discussions)

## What our AI ethics evidence covers

1. **Copyright and informed consent in training data** — creators' work should not become model training material without permission.
2. **Job displacement and the duty to retrain** — the cost of technological substitution should not fall on individuals alone.
3. **Energy and environmental cost** — the energy bill of expanding models must be measured publicly.
4. **Deepfakes and algorithmic accountability** — when a system makes a decision that affects a person, someone must be able to explain it and offer an appeal.

## Our red lines

- No harassment of individuals, no attacks on developers, no threats against any group.
- No sabotage and no offensive actions against systems.
- No unverified data — every factual claim must carry a source; see [RESEARCH/](RESEARCH/).
- No impersonating any official body or identity outside this project.

## How to participate

1. Read the [Manifesto](MANIFESTO.md) and the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
2. Start a discussion in [Discussions](https://github.com/FAI-Union/FckAI/discussions), or file evidence, a proposal, or a translation using the [issue templates](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE).
3. When submitting factual content, always include the original source. Unsourced claims are tagged `needs-source` and are not used in decisions.
4. Want to translate? Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Submissions are screened automatically for bot accounts and missing sources; see [Automated checks](CONTRIBUTING.md#automated-checks). A bot can flag a submission, but only a maintainer can reject it.
